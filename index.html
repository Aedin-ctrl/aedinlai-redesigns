Redesigns

One machine, many places. The site inside the screen stays the site — same window, same address bar, same padlock — and what changes is the room it is sitting in: the light, the surface under it, what is behind it and what is on the desk beside it. New rooms are added in batches and the weakest are retired, so the set turns over.

Nothing here yet — the first batch is being built.

Every page is gated before it is allowed to live: WCAG 2.1 AA in Chrome and in Safari at phone width, no horizontal overflow, no script errors, and motion that genuinely stops under prefers-reduced-motion rather than merely claiming to. Nothing in a room is allowed to sit over the text and cost you the reading. Links inside the pages are inert — these are designs, not a working copy of the site.
