What I actually do

I build the measuring end of things — instrumentation that has to be right, and the software that makes sense of what it reads.

A number that is quietly wrong is worse than no number. Everything downstream inherits it, every chart drawn from it looks confident, and nobody can tell by looking. Most of the job is making sure that cannot happen, and almost none of that work shows afterwards.
