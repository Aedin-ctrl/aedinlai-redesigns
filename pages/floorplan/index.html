Room 01

The bench

I build the measuring end of things — instrumentation that has to be right, and the software that makes sense of what it reads. The appeal is that the work is checkable: either the reading is the reading that happened, or it is not.

A number that is quietly wrong is worse than no number at all. Everything downstream inherits it, every chart drawn from it looks confident, and nobody can tell by looking. Almost none of the work that prevents that shows afterwards, which is the trade.

The clinic
