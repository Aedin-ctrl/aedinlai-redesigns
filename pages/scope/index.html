What the trace is for

I build the measuring end of things — instrumentation that has to be right, and the software that makes sense of what it reads. If a number on a screen is wrong, everything downstream of it is wrong and nobody can tell. That is most of the job.

Recent work runs from data centre infrastructure to research hardware for a neurology study, by way of an arcade machine built from nothing. I fence épée, the one weapon where both lights can come on at once.
